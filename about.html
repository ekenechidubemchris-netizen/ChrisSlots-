Why we built ChrisSlots

Booking a trip used to mean five different sites, five different accounts, and five different confirmation emails. ChrisSlots brings trains, buses, cabs, restaurants, hotels, cinemas, events, flights and venues into a single, consistent booking experience — so you can plan a whole trip or a single night out without leaving the app.

Every listing on ChrisSlots shows what you need to decide with confidence: real availability, transparent pricing, and reviews from people who actually booked.
