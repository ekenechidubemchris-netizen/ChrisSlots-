Help Center

Answers about booking, cancellations, payments and more.

Booking

How do I make a booking?⌄

Search a category from the homepage, choose your date, time and details, then confirm on the checkout page. You'll get a confirmation with a reference number.

Can I edit a booking after confirming it?⌄

Editable details vary by service. For now, cancel from My Bookings and create a new reservation with the updated details.

Do I need an account to book?⌄

Yes — creating a free account lets us keep your bookings, favorites and receipts in one place.

Cancellations

What's the cancellation policy?⌄

Most bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the reserved time from the My Bookings dashboard.

How do I cancel a booking?⌄

Go to My Bookings, find the reservation under Upcoming, and select Cancel.

Payment

What payment methods are supported?⌄

This demo uses a placeholder payment form. In production, ChrisSlots would connect to a real payment provider supporting major cards and digital wallets.

Is my payment information secure?⌄

No real payment details are collected or stored in this demo.
